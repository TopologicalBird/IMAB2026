Welcome to IMAB2026!

The Second International Workshop on Mathematics and AI for Biomedicine (IMAB2026) is an international workshop to communicate and interact with a wide range of researchers in the interdisciplinary field of mathematics, AI, and biomedical research. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to topology, geometry, analytical methods, physics, statistics, machine learning, big data, simulations, visualizations, computation, engineering, and technology in biomedical research. IMAB2026 will take place in hybrid mode.
