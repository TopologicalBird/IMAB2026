Welcome to IMAB2026!

The Second International Workshop on Mathematics and AI for Biomedicine (IMAB2026) is an international workshop to communicate and interact with a wide range of researchers in the interdisciplinary field of mathematics, AI, and biomedical research. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to topology, geometry, analytical methods, physics, statistics, machine learning, big data, simulations, visualizations, computation, engineering, and technology in biomedical research. IMAB2026 will take place in hybrid mode.

Message from Conference Chair

Last year, we launched our first IMAB, IMAB2025. As the fusion research field grows, we also need to grow and become a better community to support researchers. With new organizing committee members, we are planning sessions to foster active discussions and build new researcher networks. We look forward to seeing you in IMAB2026.

Looking back: IMAB2025

IMAB2025 was completed with success in UTokyo and online on December 4th, 2025, thanks to the active participation by approximately 60 participants and impressive presentations by 3 Keynote Speakers, 2 Hot Topic Speakers, and 9 Flash Talk Speakers. We hope to make IMAB2026 an even better forum for next-generation fusion research!
